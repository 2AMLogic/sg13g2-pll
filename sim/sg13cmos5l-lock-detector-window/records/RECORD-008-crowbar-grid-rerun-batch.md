## Differences from the original script's deck (all disclosed)

A request cannot substitute `@VSUP@`/`@VMID@` or read a `wrdata` trace, so:

1. The input ramp is a B-source scaled by `v(vdd)` and the mid-rail
   threshold is a B-source node `v(vmid)=v(vdd)/2`, so `corners.supply_v`
   (an `alter` of `Vdd`) moves both. The original used a fixed `pwl`.
2. The DUT cell is inlined, not `.include`d.
3. Peaks come from `.meas ... max` / `max_at` instead of scanning the
   `wrdata` trace, so the fall peak is a different estimator; that is the
   source of the 7.94 % `i_peak_fall_a` deviation (the worst-case
   `i_worst_a` is set by the rise peak at every corner, so the headline is
   unaffected).
4. **Not reproduced**: the original's `i_midband_*` (current at the midpoint
   of the two measured trip points) cannot be a deck `.meas` because a
   `.meas` cannot reference another `.meas` result at parse time (first
   attempt failed with `Undefined parameter [vth_up]`). The rerun CSV carries
   `i_vdd2_rise_a` / `i_vdd2_fall_a` (current with the input at mid-rail)
   instead; the two quantities are different and are not compared.
5. `v_at_i_peak_*` are derived from the peak time, `i_worst_a` from the two
   peaks, in `post_rerun331.py`; `i_rail_*` are taken at `t=0` and `t=2us`,
   not at the nearest `vin` sample, and are reported as magnitudes.
