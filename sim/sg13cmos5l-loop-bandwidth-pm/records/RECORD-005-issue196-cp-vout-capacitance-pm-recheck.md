## Method

Both benches are generated by `../testbench/gen_issue196_ccp_requests.py` and
reduced by `../testbench/summarize_issue196.py`.

### 1. Idle cp capacitance on VOUT: `tb_cp_cout_issue196.sp`

For 14 VOUT points (0.3 to 2.9 V, 0.2 V steps), four idle cp instances
(UP = DN = 0, the state the loop filter sees between reference edges) sit on
one AC source. Each has its own 0 V ammeter:

- the real `cp` at the 3.75 uA (DR-007), 10 uA and 11 uA (DR-008) codes;
- `cp_idealdump` at 10 uA. This is the snapshot's `cp` with `XBUF` replaced
  by an ideal VCVS, as in cp-icp-trim's `tb_cp_otabuf_cin.sp`. It is the legs,
  switches and bias replica without the buffer.

Nothing else loads the source, so `imag(i)/omega` is the **total** cp
capacitance ("real cp vs no cp"). That is the quantity cp-icp-trim's
buffer-only bench could not give. `c10 - b10` is the buffer-only part.

### 2. PM with the real cp on VCTRL: `tb_loop_ac_ccp_issue196.sp`

This uses option 2 of the issue: the real `cp` subckt, idle, on the
loop-filter node. It is not a lumped estimate, so the cp's whole small-signal
admittance (C and G) is in the loop, at the tuple's own trim code. For each
of the four tuples the deck holds:

- `<t>_nocp`: the `RECORD-003`/`004` loop verbatim (ideal `Gcp`, no cp). This
  is the regression anchor.
- `<t>_lonly`: the VCTRL bias network alone (1 TH inductor to a 1.5 V source,
  no cp). This control must equal `_nocp`.
- `<t>_v<V>`: the loop plus the real idle cp, with VCTRL biased to V through
  the inductor, for the same 14 points. At 400 kHz the inductor is 2.5e15 Ohm
  against R1 = 344 kOhm. Its resonance with C1 + C2 is about 0.1 Hz, below
  the 10 Hz sweep start. The VCO integrator is driven by `v(vc) - v(vb)`. `vb`
  has no AC, so the AC loop is unchanged, but without this the DC bias winds
  the ideal integrator to about 1e23 V and the operating point fails. That
  happened on the first attempt and was fixed before any result was taken.

PM = 180 deg + `vp(fb)` at `|fb| = 0 dB` (ngspice `.meas`, linear
interpolation; the sweep is `dec 200 10 1e9` as before).

**Which VCTRL is graded.** Each tuple's Kvco is the secant of its own
interval (`../corners/matrix.md`): `low` is 0.3 to 0.9 V and `mid` is 0.9 to
1.5 V. So the loop really sits inside that interval. Two verdicts are reported
per tuple: the worst case over the tuple's **own interval** points, and the
worst case over the **full 0.3-2.9 V** range, as the issue's test plan asks.
The full-range figure is the more conservative of the two.

**Every corner evaluates every tuple's loops.** A `klt sim` request has one
netlist and cannot bind per-corner `.param` values to a process bundle (see
2AMLogic/klayout-tools#2725). So all four tuples' loops are in one deck, and
a tuple's result is read only from its own bundle's corner. The off-bundle
loops at the `typ` corner are used below only as a labelled sensitivity
estimate. They are never used as a verdict.

**Bundles**, as in `RECORD-002`..`004`: `fast` = `mos_ff` + `res_bcs` at
-40 C, `typ` = `mos_tt` + `res_typ` at 27 C, `slow` = `mos_ss` + `res_wcs` at
125 C.

At `typ` the real cp costs DR-008's tuple **-0.653 deg** over the `low`
interval and **-0.812 deg** over the full range. The tuple still passes with
3.2-3.4 deg of margin. f_c falls from 385151 to at most 383449 Hz, 14.8% under

**The regression anchor holds.** For `dr008_typ`, `_nocp` gives 49.043 deg /
385151 Hz against `RECORD-004`'s 49.043 deg / 385147.8 Hz. The 3 Hz difference
is `.meas` linear interpolation versus the old script's log interpolation.
The bias-only control gives 49.043 deg, so the bias network is invisible.

**Sanity check against the issue's analytic estimate.** That estimate was
about -0.9 deg for +16 fF at f_c = 0.5 MHz. This bench gives -0.81 deg at
f_c = 0.38 MHz with up to 16.6 fF (`typ`, full range). The two agree in sign
and size. In every case f_c moves *down* (by about 0.4-0.5%), so the
450 kHz ceiling only gains headroom.

## Verdict flips

**No flip was measured.** The one tuple that could be measured (`typ`) passes
before and after.

**One flip is projected: DR-008's `fast` tuple.** It closed with +0.592 deg
of margin (45.592 deg). The `dr008_fast` loops were also evaluated at the
`typ` corner, with the `fast` tuple's own Kd, Kv and N but `typ` models and
the `typ` cp. They lose **-0.658 deg** (own interval) and **-0.818 deg**
(full range) to the cp. Applied to the measured `fast` baseline, that
projects **about 44.93 deg (own interval) and 44.77 deg (full range)**, both
below the 45 deg floor. This is an estimate, not a verdict. The `fast`
corner's own models and cp capacitance were not simulated. It is filed as
**#203** (a measured `fast`/`slow` grid, then a new decision record if it
fails). DR-008 is not edited here.

The other blocked tuples project well clear. They use the same off-bundle
sensitivities (`dr008_slow` -0.848 deg, `dr007_slow` -0.705 deg, full range),
with the same caveat:

| Tuple | Before (deg) | Sensitivity at `typ` models, full range (deg) | Projected (deg) | Projected verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DR-008 `fast` | 45.592 | -0.818 (own interval -0.658) | 44.77 (44.93) | **fail (projected flip)** |
| DR-008 `slow` | 53.454 | -0.848 (own interval -0.682) | 52.61 | pass |
| DR-007 `slow` | 49.973 | -0.705 (own interval -0.470) | 49.27 | pass |

f_c moves down in every loop (by 0.4-0.5% at `typ`), so the f_c < f_ref/10
criterion cannot newly fail because of the cp. This matters most for DR-008
`slow`, which had only 3.28% of headroom.

## Spec-row disposition

- **Row 6a is not relaxed.** DR-007 and DR-008 are not edited.
- **Row 6/6a, DR-008 band 00 / low / f_ref 4.5 MHz, `typ`**: passes with
  the real cp's idle capacitance in the loop (48.231 deg worst case,
  schematic-level).
- **DR-008 `fast` and `slow`, and DR-007 `slow`: not re-verified.** They are
  blocked on the batch runner (klayout-tools#2851 / #2727). DR-008 `fast` is
  **projected to fail** row 6a by about 0.07-0.23 deg once the cp is
  included. Until #203 lands a measured grid, `RECORD-004`'s `fast` closure
  should be read as resting on the ideal-Gcp model and as at risk.

## What this does not bound

- **Layout parasitics.** Everything here is schematic-level (see Scope).
  `pll_cp`'s routed VOUT net and the DR-010 redraw (#195) add capacitance on
  the same node. The post-layout PM is owed to the PEX flow.
- **Supply corners and Monte Carlo.** VDD is 3.3 V throughout. No mismatch
  sampling was run.
- **The sampled-loop and dynamic cp effects.** This is still a linear,
  continuous-time open-loop AC bench. The cp's charge-sharing and switching
  terms are closed-loop transient territory
  (`sim/sg13cmos5l-closed-loop-lock`).
- **The other 28 (band, interval, f_ref) combinations of `RECORD-002`.** They
  were not re-run. Their PM margins are wider than the sub-1-deg ones
  re-checked here, but none is re-verified by this record.
