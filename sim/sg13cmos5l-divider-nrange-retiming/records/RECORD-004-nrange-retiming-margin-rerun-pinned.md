## Reconstruction of the historical DUT and decks

`../netlist-snapshots/divider_chain.spice` is byte-identical at `1d824d3`
(RECORD-001), `b8a14c8` (RECORD-002) and at the base commit (sha256
`9cfc67ce1b8691e34d23c2b7f04a7e741d74c58465a67e1fd8d9113624355f6d`), so the
pre-repair as-drawn DUT is the committed snapshot. The `fbfix` proposal
variant is derived from it with the historical `run.sh`'s three-line edit,
copied verbatim into `../testbench/rerun232/gen_requests.py` (sha256 of the
derived `divider_chain_fbfix.spice`
`8470c44707edbbcd661b193e5b5140e870e19ec7eaf57c157718e822ba1adae5`). The
repaired divider (`divider_chain_repaired.spice`, RECORD-003's DUT) is **not**
used. The historical `.sp.tmpl` decks and `run.sh` stages were re-expressed
as `klt sim` requests (`../testbench/rerun232/*.request.json` with netlist
bodies `*.sp`, generated by `gen_requests.py`; reports converted by
`reports_to_csv.py`). This is a **port of the harness**, not a byte-for-byte
rerun of `run.sh`: the same stimuli, `.ic` symmetry break, tolerances
(`reltol=5e-3` for whole-chain decks), measurement windows and the same
9-point (setup: 3-bundle) matrix, but measurements are `.meas` cards instead
of `meas` commands and the supply is a `corners.params` value. No reduced
corner was added.

## Result 1 (Finding 2): hold path

`../corners/rerun232-hold.csv`, 18 rows. As-drawn `q_end`/`s_end` settle to
1.378-1.687 V in every one of 9 corners (nominal 1.53085 V; original
1.531), `s_end` identical to `q_end`; `fbfix` holds at the rail in 9/9
(`q_end` = 3.300 / 2.970 / 3.630 V, the corner supply). **Reproduced** for all
18 rows to the original's printed precision (e.g. `mos_fs` 1.4818 vs 1.482,
`mos_tt`/27 C/2.97 V 1.37766 vs 1.378). `t_decay` is `NA` in every row, as in
the original; `klt` grades that `.meas` "no value" as a measurement `error`
(`out of interval`), which for `fbfix` is the pass condition (Q never leaves the rail); for as-drawn the cause was not examined (Q is already mid-rail), so those reports carry `status: error` although the stage is as expected.

## Result 2 (Finding 3): `fbfix` setup capture, and the as-drawn curve

`../corners/rerun232-setup.csv`, 36 rows. `fbfix` `q_sample` stays within
+-31 mV of 0 V at every tested `TSU` (300 ps ... 0) at all 3 bundles
(nominal 300 ps: -0.01638 V vs original -0.016; ss/125 C: -0.00942 vs -0.009;
ff/-40 C: -0.03077 vs -0.031): **reproduced**, so the original's conclusion that
the retiming margin at 1562.0 MHz is inadequate *for this weak-keeper sizing*
is supported again. As-drawn nominal: 3.04858, 3.04504, 3.00388, 2.81378,
2.71841, 0.553846 V (original 3.05, 3.05, 3.00, 2.81, 2.72, 0.55): reproduced.

## Result 3 (Finding 1): operating-point convergence — NOT reproduced

`../corners/rerun232-opconv.csv`:

| variant | ic | rerun | original |
|---|---|---|---|
| asdrawn | none | converged, `v(divout)` = 1.84696 V, ngspice warning `Dynamic gmin stepping failed` | `converged=no` (no result in 150 s) |
| asdrawn | applied | converged, 1.84696 V, same warning | `converged=no` |
| fbfix | none | converged, 3.30000 V | `converged=no` (superseded for `fbfix` by RECORD-002) |
| fbfix | applied | converged, 3.30000 V | `converged=no` (same) |

The original's headline claim, that the as-drawn chain's bare `.op` does not
converge within 150 s with or without `.ic` (and the hand observation of a
5-minute stall), is **not reproduced**: each as-drawn run finished in 4-5 s of
simulator time. The same ngspice warning RECORD-001 describes
(`gmin stepping failed`) does appear, but a later fallback succeeds and lands
on a mid-rail `v(divout)`, the self-biased-inverter signature of Finding 2. No
cause for the difference is established; the simulator build (ngspice-46 batch
worker), host, and model-tree revision all differ or are unknown for the
original, so this rerun cannot say whether the original's stall was the
design, the host, or the toolchain. The `.op` is run as `analysis.kind: op`,
not as the original's `ngspice -b` `op` card; they exercise the same solver
path but are not byte-identical decks. The original finding remains in
`RECORD-001`; this record neither confirms nor refutes the 5-minute stall, it
only reports that the batch worker did not reproduce it.

## Result 4 (Finding 4): 100 MHz baseline, word `000000`, nominal corner only

`../corners/rerun232-func_baseline.csv`:

| Variant | rerun | original |
|---|---|---|
| `fbfix`, `mos_tt`/27 C/3.3 V | all five stages and `DIVOUT`/`FB` rail-to-rail; `tdiv_a` = 6.54319e-7 s, `tdiv_b` = 1.29433e-6 s, so 640.011 ns = **N = 64.00**; `idd` = -165.768 uA | N = 64.00 (640.012 ns), `idd` = -165.76 uA |
| as-drawn, `mos_tt`/27 C/3.3 V | `ck1_max` 2.064 V, `dvo_max` 0.482 V, `idd` = -7.765 mA; `DIVOUT` 50 % crossings at 10.0 ns and 22.5 ns (about one to two CKIN cycles apart, not 640 ns) | dead: 0 V everywhere, `idd` = 0 (7 of 9 corners) |

`fbfix` is **reproduced** (N = 64.00, `idd` agrees to 0.01 uA). The original's
"reproducibility trouble" for this deck (converge in minutes or not in
400-700 s) was not seen: it ran in 88.6 s of simulator time. For the as-drawn
chain the original's "dead state, 0 V everywhere" is **not reproduced** at
nominal; this rerun shows internal toggling and 7.8 mA of supply current. It
does reproduce the conclusion that the as-drawn chain does not divide by 64:
the measured `DIVOUT` edges are one to two input cycles apart, not N = 64.
The original described two of its nine as-drawn corners as showing partial
toggling; only nominal is rerun here, so this is a difference at a corner the
original called dead, not at the two it called anomalous.

## Result 5 (Finding 5): top-of-band retiming at 1562.0 MHz, nominal only

`../corners/rerun232-retime.csv`:

| Variant | rerun | original |
|---|---|---|
| `fbfix`, nominal | `tdiv` = 1.07744e-7 s, `tfb` = 1.08684e-7 s, `dvo_max` = 3.60125 V, `fb_max` = 3.42414 V, `idd` = -1.3076 mA (66.1 s) | `insufficient-evidence` for a clean two-edge number; manual log `dvo_max` 3.598 V, `fb_max` 3.424 V, `idd` -1.287 mA |
| as-drawn, nominal | `tdiv` = 5.765e-9 s, `tfb` = 3.946e-9 s, `dvo_max` = 1.752 V, `idd` = -8.397 mA | all `NA` (0 of 4 corners functional) |

The `fbfix` values are real, converged `.meas` results, so the "no clean
crossing could be obtained" part of the original is **not reproduced**: a
crossing now exists. The original's other statement stands unchanged: **no
numeric retiming margin is claimed.** The two timestamps are the second 50 %
crossings of two different signals during a settling transient, they are 1 ns
apart, and the earliest genuine divide-by-64 edge cannot occur before 41 ns
(64 x 640.2 ps); the record does not interpret them as `setup_avail` or
`clk->Q`, and the qualitative "inadequate margin" conclusion rests on Result 2
as before. The as-drawn `tdiv`/`tfb` (5.8 ns, 3.9 ns, below 41 ns) are not
divide-by-64 edges and are not evidence of function, only of a non-`NA`
`.meas` result on a chain that does not divide. They differ from the
original's all-`NA` row.

## Limitations retained

- Corner coverage as listed under "Coverage": baseline and retime are nominal
  only; 8 corners each remain unmeasured by any new run.
- The ported harness is not the historical `run.sh`; measurements are `.meas`
  cards. The historical `meas` commands were not run.
- The batch worker's own PDK tree revision is not stamped; model-file hashes
  match the dispatching host's.
- Schematic-level only; ideal sources; no mismatch, layout parasitics or aging.
- `reltol` was not swept; the original's `tol_convergence` data does not exist
  and none is added.
- The original's host was not stated and no log exists, so nothing here proves
  the original's environment.
