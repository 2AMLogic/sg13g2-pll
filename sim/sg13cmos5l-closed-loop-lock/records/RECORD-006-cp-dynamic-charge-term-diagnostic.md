# RECORD-006: the 8.2% static phase error is dump-node charge sharing in `cp`, not switch timing or the PFD reset window

- **Slug**: `sg13cmos5l-closed-loop-lock`
- **Issue**: #150 (Part of #16). This continues the #70 / #72 chain and
  `RECORD-005`'s own "Follow-up" section: it isolates the remaining dynamic
  term that `RECORD-005` named as its best candidate but did not isolate.
- **Append-only.** This record does not edit `RECORD-001` through `RECORD-005`,
  any snapshot under `../netlist-snapshots/`, any existing CSV under
  `../corners/`, any existing script under `../testbench/`, anything under
  `design/`, or any decision record. Every artifact it cites is new and named
  `cpdiag_*` / `cppulse_*` / `lock_trace_cpdiag_*`.
- **What this is, and what it is not.** This is a nominal-corner,
  diagnostic-only study run on `RECORD-005`'s proposal deck. It keeps every
  assumption that deck makes: a behavioural XSPICE divide-by-64 instead of
  the committed `divider_chain`, `R1` resized from 120u to 2400u (`rppd`),
  ideal-capacitor substitutions for every MOM cap, `XCDECAP` stripped from
  `vco`, and ideal `IREF` current sources into `cp`'s bias pins. **Every
  number below is a proposal-deck diagnostic number, not committed-PLL
  performance and not sign-off evidence.** The variants that close the gap
  use ideal elements (VCVS, ideal switches) that are not a design.

## Result in one paragraph

The mitigated `cp`'s dump buffer `XBUF` (`cp_dumpbuf`, an NMOS source
follower from `VOUT`) holds `VDUMP` about **0.94 V below `VOUT`**: 1.4496 V
against `VOUT` = 2.387 V at the operating point. While a leg is steered to
the dump branch, its switch-common node `sw` (the drain of cascode `XM2`,
shared by `XSWO`/`XSWD`) therefore sits near `VDUMP`: 1.479 V on the P leg,
1.448 V on the N leg. At every `UP` or `DN` turn-on that node has to be
pulled up roughly 0.92-0.94 V to `VOUT`, and the charge comes **out of the
loop filter**. This happens for **both** legs, with the **same sign**, so it
is not an up/dn mismatch that better current matching could cancel (which
is why DR-006's 20x DC-matching fix barely moved the error). The open-loop
bench measures **-41.30 fC per UP+DN pulse pair**, independent of pulse
width (fit residual 0.10 fC; slope 0.06 uA). The loop can only replace that
charge with an UP-only lead. The predicted lead is 41.30 fC / 10.049 uA =
4.110 ns, which is **8.220% of T_ref**; the closed loop measures a 4.116 ns
median lead and **8.204%**. Replacing `XBUF` with an ideal unity follower
(`VDUMP = VOUT`) cuts the per-cycle charge to -8.10 fC, and the closed loop
then settles to **1.048%** with a **45-cycle dual-lock run** and **lock time
250 ns**. An ideal follower that **keeps** the 0.937 V level shift restores
the open-loop charge to -44.70 fC and the closed-loop error to **7.395%**.
So the dump node's **DC level** accounts for 6.35 of the 7.16 percentage
points that ideal tracking removes (about 89%). The remaining about 0.8 pp
is a second-order contribution from the real buffer's own dynamics in the
closed loop. Ideal steering switches with
the real dump buffer only reach 5.183%, and changing the PFD reset window by
0.86x / 1.17x moves the error by at most 0.012 percentage points. **Row 7
still fails for the committed and mitigated design**: nothing here changes
`cp.sch`.

## Fixed conditions (identical for every closed-loop run, identical to RECORD-005)

`mos_tt` / `res_typ`, 27 C, all supplies 3.3 V, `REF` 20 MHz (100 ps
edges), N = 64 (behavioural), `IREF` = 10 uA into `IBP`/`ICP`/`IBN`/`ICN`,
`.ic v(vctrl)=2.46`, `.ic v(xvco.ring1)=0.5`, `B0`=`B1`=3.3 V,
`.tran 100p 2500n 0 100p`. Supply-current and control-voltage `.meas` window
2.0-2.5 us, as in RECORD-005. The lock metrics use RECORD-005's own
`lock_trace` extractor, ported into `../testbench/cpdiag_metrics.py` with the
same definition (only an unused index loop was dropped)
(edge threshold 1.65 V on the linearized 100 ps `v(ref)`/`v(fb)`).

- phase error = (nearest FB rising edge - REF rising edge) / T_ref; positive
  means FB lags REF, i.e. UP leads;
- delta f / f_ref per reference cycle;
- final-20-cycle statistics over the last 20 of 49 cycles (t > 1.5 us);
- longest consecutive dual-lock run: |delta f/f_ref| < 1% AND |phase| < 5%,
  both strict;
- lock time: the first cycle of the first run of at least 20 consecutive
  dual-lock cycles, reported as `None` when absent (never 0).

What this bench shows:

1. **The control's per-cycle error charge is a width-independent
   intercept, not a current mismatch.** For UP+DN pulses, Q0 = -41.30 fC
   and the slope is +0.062 uA. Over 1.6-6.4 ns of overlap (a 4x range in
   reset window), the width term contributes 0.06 fC/ns, about 0.15% of Q0
   per ns. That bounds the reset-window mechanism directly and agrees with
   the closed-loop (c) results.
2. **It is the same sign for UP and for DN** (-19.98 and -22.82 fC), and
   UP+DN superposes to within 1.5 fC. Both turn-ons pull charge from `VOUT`.
   A DC up/dn matching fix cannot cancel a same-sign term.
3. **It scales with the step the `sw` nodes see at turn-on.** Off-state
   levels are sw_p = 1.479 V and sw_n = 1.448 V (both near `VDUMP` =
   1.450 V); on-state levels are 2.395 V and 2.383 V (near `VOUT`). The step
   is 0.917 V (P) and 0.935 V (N), i.e. an effective 21.8 fF (P) and 24.4 fF
   (N) of `sw`-node capacitance per leg. With `VDUMP = VOUT` (b) the step
   is 0 V and Q0 falls 5x, to -8.10 fC. That -8.10 fC is the real switches'
   own injection and feedthrough at equal node levels; it disappears with
   ideal switches as well (a)+(b) (+0.40 fC).
4. **Level first, dynamics second.** (b') keeps the ideal follower of (b)
   but restores the 0.937 V shift. With `VOUT` held ideal, Q0 returns to
   -44.70 fC, slightly *worse* than the real buffer, whose finite output
   impedance lets `VDUMP` absorb part of the transient. In the closed loop
   (b') gives 7.395% against the control's 8.204%, so there the real
   buffer's dynamics add about 0.8 pp (about 4 fC per cycle) on top of the
   level term. Two observations point to how. First, in the closed loop
   `VOUT` is not held: each turn-on kicks it, since `C2` is only 100 fF.
   Second, the real follower's gate sits on `VOUT`, so `VDUMP`'s
   post-kick settling couples back. The control's -26 nA post-pulse `i_off`
   tail is present only with the real buffer: (b) gives -0.12 nA and (b')
   -0.07 nA. Which of these dominates is not separated further here. The
   open-loop bench cannot show this term because it holds `VOUT` with an
   ideal source.
5. **(a) ideal switches leave most of it in place** (-25.45 fC). The ideal
   switch removes the switch devices' own charge, but the `sw` node still
   carries the large cascode `XM2` drain (P: 24u/1u, N: 8u/1u) swinging
   about 0.94 V.
6. **Quantitative closure:** -Q0_both / (I_up T_ref) predicts 8.220 / 5.065
   / 1.620 / 8.896 % for control / (a) / (b) / (b'). The closed loop
   measures 8.204 / 5.183 / 1.048 / 7.395 %. The control and (a) agree
   within 0.12 pp. For (b) the closed loop beats the prediction, as expected:
   with only a 0.53 ns lead the UP and DN turn-on transients overlap, so the
   isolated-pulse intercept over-counts. For (b') the closed loop is 1.5 pp
   below the held-`VOUT` prediction. The ideal follower tracks `VOUT`'s
   per-event kicks instantly, which a held `VOUT` never exercises; see
   point 4. The open-loop prediction is therefore a first-order estimate;
   the closed-loop numbers are the measured result.
7. **Bias/branch currents are not what changed.** UP plateau is 10.049 /
   10.049 / 10.000 / 10.049 uA, and DN is -10.137 / -10.235 / -10.027 /
   -9.987 uA across control / (a) / (b) / (b'). (b) and (b') share the same
   bias-branch removal and almost the same plateaus, but their Q0 differs by
   36.6 fC. The quiet output current is fA-level in every variant, so the
   off-state leakage contribution is negligible.

## Determinism and checks

- **Repeats:** `control_hist` vs `control_hist_rep` (closed loop),
  `ideal_dump` vs `ideal_dump_rep` (closed loop, the decisive variant: trace
  **and** per-cycle CSV), and `control_probe` vs `control_probe_rep`
  (open loop) are all **byte-identical**. An earlier, interrupted session of
  this same sweep had already run `control_hist`, `control_probe`,
  `ideal_sw`, `ideal_dump` (closed loop) and five open-loop variants with the
  same simulator binary. Its outputs were byte-identical to this session's,
  and they were deleted rather than committed twice.
- **Simulator exit status 0** for every committed run. All `vctrl` samples
  are finite (`"finite": true`). There are 50 REF and 50 FB rising edges per
  closed-loop run, giving 49 cycles. In each probe variant, 50 UP/DN pulse
  pairs give 49 complete per-cycle rows in its per-cycle CSV. The 2.5 us window
  covers the final 20 cycles (t > 1.5 us) in every variant.
- **One run had to be repeated for a procedural reason, not a numeric
  one.** The first `ideal_dump_ofs` closed-loop run completed ngspice but
  its post-processing aborted (`unexpected EOF`), because a header comment
  of `run_cp_dynamic_diag.sh` was edited while that invocation was still
  executing. No output from it was kept. The committed `ideal_dump_ofs`
  results are from a clean re-run of the final script.
- **Metric extraction self-test:** `python3 -I ../testbench/test_cpdiag_metrics.py`
  (stdlib only) passes. It covers no lock, a 19-cycle run (no lock time), a
  20-cycle run (lock time = first cycle of the run), a run split by one bad
  cycle, strict-inequality boundaries (|phase| = 5% and |df| = 1% never
  count, and non-finite values never count), an empty trace, the final-20
  statistics, phase extraction on synthetic square waves (8% lag -> no lock,
  3% -> lock), and `pulse_cycles` on synthetic rectangles. The rectangle
  check covers lead/overlap/charge partitioning, the sign convention (a
  -50 fC spike shows up as `q_edge` = -50 fC) and the off-state-only case.
- **Charge-probe sign and windows:** sign verified by the open-loop UP-only
  and DN-only plateau currents (+10.05 / -10.14 uA). Closed-loop window
  bookkeeping is verified by `q_cycle` ~ 0 in steady state. The open-loop
  quiet window (20-90 ns, both pulses low) gives fA-level leakage.

## Conclusion

**Supported mechanism:** dump-node charge sharing. `cp_dumpbuf`, an NMOS
source follower, holds `VDUMP` one V_GS (about 0.94 V) below `VOUT`, so
each leg's switch-common node `sw` idles about 0.94 V below `VOUT`. At every
UP and every DN turn-on, about 20-23 fC per leg flows out of the loop filter
to charge that node. Both legs act in the same direction, about 41 fC per
reference cycle in total, and the loop cancels it with a 4.1 ns UP lead:
8.2% of T_ref. This accounts for the closed-loop error to within 0.02 pp.
It is consistent with every variant:

- the PFD reset window does not matter (c);
- ideal switches remove only the switch devices' share (a);
- ideal `VDUMP = VOUT` removes about 80% of the open-loop charge and closes
  the nominal phase error to 1.05% with lock (b);
- restoring only the DC level shift brings back 6.35 of the 7.16 pp (b'),
  giving 7.395% with no lock.

Two second-order terms remain. About 0.8 pp in the closed loop comes from
the real buffer's own dynamics, (b') vs control. About 8 fC open-loop at
equal levels is the real switches' own injection and feedthrough, (b) vs
(a)+(b). Either would be the next term after the dump level is fixed.

This narrows `RECORD-005`'s candidate. RECORD-005 called it "switching /
dynamic charge mismatch". It is a dynamic charge term, but the dominant part
is set by a **static** node-voltage offset in the dump branch, not by switch
timing, switch size, or up/dn asymmetry. That also explains why DR-006's
DC-matching fix moved the error only about 11%.

**Proposed design change (separately scoped, not done here):** make the dump
node track `VOUT` at DC. One option is to replace `cp_dumpbuf`'s source
follower with a unity-gain amplifier (OTA in unity feedback, whose input
common-mode range must cover the `VOUT` operating range inside the 3.3 V
rail box). Another is a level-shift-compensated follower. Either is a
`design/sg13cmos5l/cp.sch` change and needs its own issue, a decision record,
and full-PVT re-verification (`cp-icp-trim` DC matching plus this
closed-loop deck via `klt sim` batch dispatch). The (b) result bounds the
benefit at nominal: about 1.05% with an ideal tracker. A real amplifier's
offset will add back about (offset / 0.94 V) x 33 fC. Switch-charge
cancellation (the about 8 fC residual) is a possible second step, not a
first one.

**No rail/spec conflict is established.** The (b) variant meets row 7's 5%
threshold at nominal with ideal tracking, so this evidence routes to a
design change, not a decision record. Nothing here assesses whether a real
tracker fits the rail box across PVT.

## Row-by-row disposition (does not edit RECORD-001 through RECORD-005)

- **Row 7 (lock time / static phase error): still FAILS** for the committed
  and the DR-006-mitigated design. This record's control reproduces
  RECORD-005's 8.20% and 4-cycle result. The variant that passes, (b), uses
  an ideal VCVS in place of a real block and is a diagnostic, not a design.
  It is also on the proposal deck (behavioural divider, resized R1, ideal
  caps). The 5% criterion is not revisited or relaxed.
- **Rows 10 and 11**: no new evidence. The `i_cp` values above are
  diagnostic-deck values for variants that remove or alter real devices. They
  supersede nothing in RECORD-005's row-11 figure.

## What this does not bound

- **One PVT point** (`mos_tt`/`res_typ`/27 C/3.3 V). The source-follower
  V_GS, and with it the offset, varies with corner and temperature, so the
  error size will too. A full-PVT re-run belongs with the design change and
  goes through `klt sim` (batch backend) per host policy. That route is
  currently blocked for this PDK: `klt sim`'s batch backend refuses
  `ihp-sg13cmos5l` (klayout-tools#2727, already cited in `sim/README.md`).
  No corner grid was run here.
- **Proposal deck only** (behavioural divide-by-64, R1 2400u, ideal caps,
  ideal `IREF`). The committed `divider_chain` loop (Part A) is not
  exercised.
- **Ideal elements are not designs.** The 1.05% of (b) is an idealized
  bound, not a prediction for any particular dump amplifier.
- **The open-loop bench holds `VOUT` with an ideal source.** It measures
  charge per pulse, not loop dynamics. Its phase prediction is a first-order
  estimate (it over-predicts (b) by 0.57 pp, for the reason given above).
- **Reset factors are modest** (overlap 0.86x / 1.17x in closed loop). The
  open-loop UP+DN width sweep (1.6-6.4 ns) covers a 4x range and agrees.
